## Task
You receive one candidate topic (a cluster of related signals). Score it from 1 to 5 on each
of the five criteria below, with a one-line justification per criterion, and write `why_now`.
Score only; do not compute totals; do not state statistics you were not given.

## Criteria
- `data_ability`: Can this be charted with real, obtainable numbers?
  - 1: no public numbers exist, or only anecdotes and opinion.
  - 5: an official, regularly published series (KNBS, CBK, EPRA, Treasury) covers it directly.
- `wallet_impact`: Does it touch everyday Kenyan money?
  - 1: no effect on household or small-business money.
  - 5: directly changes what most households pay or earn (fuel, unga, rent, fees, taxes).
- `timeliness`: Why now? Is there a release, debate or deadline?
  - 1: no hook; it could have run any time this year.
  - 5: a release, vote or deadline lands within days.
- `clarity_gap`: Is there confusion or misinformation we can clear up?
  - 1: the facts are already clear and widely understood.
  - 5: readers are visibly confused or misled and a clear chart would settle it.
- `novelty`: Have we not covered this recently?
  - 1: heavily covered already, with nothing new to add.
  - 5: little or no recent coverage; a fresh angle.

## Output
- `justification` maps each criterion name to one short sentence.
- `why_now` is at most 25 words.
- Each score is an integer from 1 to 5.
